SRS — Family Tea Cashier Management

THE PROBLEM

Family Tea's daily operations rely entirely on manual logbooks. This leads to slow transaction services, high human error rates in revenue tracking, and discrepancies in daily financial reports.

THE SOLUTION

Designing a Web-Based Point of Sale (POS) System with a minimalist, user-friendly interface. Key features include quick order input, cash outflow tracking, transaction voiding, and automated net profit reports.
